04 · How the agents work

The Royal Council: two AI agents, two code agents

Fairy Godmothergpt-6-luna styles one outfit from a shortlist of legal products; returns IDs and reasons as JSON.
Royal TreasurerCode re-prices every item and checks 9 hard rules; sends itemized rejections back.
Wicked StepmotherCode picks one of 8 reproducible crises that really threatens this outfit.
Midnight Guardiangpt-6-luna repairs only what broke; a validator rejects any rule break or needless swap.

Why this split: arithmetic and stock are facts, so code makes them exact, free and instant. The model does what needs taste. Caps: 6 model calls per request, 2 revisions per agent.

07 · Safety

What could go wrong, and the defenses that caught it

Prompt injection · data leaks
Notes scanned and withheld if they contain instructions; API key only on the server
Invented products or prices
Model outputs IDs only; facts come from retailer data; unknown IDs rejected
Wrong actions · fake success
Validator gates every outfit and swap; impossible requests refused honestly
Infinite loops · runaway spending
Max 6 calls per request, 60 s timeouts, $3/day cap, rate limit

Caught in testing: the single-AI baseline shipped 6 invalid “rescues” out of 17. The council’s validator blocked every one.

09 · What's next

Limits today, and what users asked for next

Limits today

  • Prices and stock are dated snapshots, not a partner feed
  • Style attributes hand-labeled for 133 products
  • Sizes shown, not used for styling
  • 8 predefined crises; small evidence base

Next, from user testing

  • Upload a photo of your own clothes and style around it
  • More aesthetics: old money, dark academia, minimalist
  • Everyday casual mode and a clearer first click
  • Always check the weather; partner feeds for live stock
